# 100 Problem-Solution Fit Interview Questions

## How to Conduct These Interviews (The "Mom Test" Rules)
1. **Talk about their life, not your idea:** Do not mention "Craft Atelier" until the very end. If you pitch your idea too early, they will just be polite and say it sounds great.
2. **Ask about the past, not the future:** People are terrible at predicting their future behavior. Instead of asking "Would you buy this?", ask "Tell me about the last time you bought something like this."
3. **Listen for specifics:** If they say "It's hard to find yarn," ask "How much time did you spend looking last week?"

## Segment 1: The "Craft-Curious" Beginner (B2C) (Questions 1-25)
**Focus: Leisure time, stress, abandoned hobbies, and learning friction.**
1. Walk me through a typical weekday evening. How do you usually unwind?
2. Have you ever tried to reduce your screen time? What did you replace it with?
3. Tell me about the last time you tried to learn a new physical skill or hobby.
4. What was the exact trigger that made you want to learn that hobby?
5. Did you stick with it? If not, what was the exact moment you gave up?
6. Have you ever attempted knitting, crochet, or pottery? 
7. Walk me through the day you decided to buy supplies for a DIY project. Where did you go?
8. How long did it take you to gather all the necessary materials?
9. Did you buy the right things on the first try?
10. Tell me about the last time you watched a YouTube tutorial to make something physical.
11. What was the most frustrating part of following along with that video?
12. Have you ever bought a pattern online? How did reading it make you feel?
13. Tell me about a time you bought a craft supply and never used it. Why is it sitting in a closet?
14. How much money would you say you wasted on abandoned hobby supplies last year?
15. Do you prefer learning in a physical class or at home? Why?
16. Tell me about the last time you felt a deep sense of accomplishment outside of work.
17. Have you ever looked for a curated "all-in-one" kit for anything? (e.g., HelloFresh for food).
18. What do you hate most about going to traditional craft or fabric stores?
19. Have you ever gifted a handmade item to someone? Walk me through that process.
20. When you look at crochet items on Instagram, what goes through your mind?
21. What is the biggest barrier preventing you from starting a project today?
22. Tell me about the last time a delivery took too long. How did it impact your motivation?
23. If you could instantly learn one physical craft tomorrow, what would it be?
24. Have you ever felt embarrassed or overwhelmed asking questions in a hobby store?
25. How important is the aesthetics/branding of the supplies you buy?

## Segment 2: Corporate / Event Buyers (B2B) (Questions 26-50)
**Focus: Remote team morale, logistics pain, budget allocation, and stale events.**
26. Walk me through how your company currently handles employee wellness or morale.
27. Tell me about the last team-building event you organized. How did it go?
28. What was the feedback from the employees after that event?
29. How has managing remote or hybrid teams changed your approach to company culture?
30. What is the biggest complaint you hear from employees about Zoom/virtual events?
31. Tell me about the last time you sent a physical gift to your employees.
32. Walk me through the exact logistical process of getting those gifts to 50 different houses.
33. How much time did you personally spend managing addresses, tracking numbers, and lost packages?
34. Have you ever had a corporate gifting initiative go completely wrong? What happened?
35. How do you decide the budget per head for an employee appreciation gift?
36. Who has to approve that budget, and what ROI do they expect to see?
37. Have you ever used a "snack box" or "swag bag" service? What was missing from that experience?
38. Do your employees prefer consumable gifts (food) or experiential gifts (activities)? How do you know?
39. Tell me about a time you struggled to find a vendor for a corporate event.
40. How important is it that a corporate gift has your company's logo on it?
41. If you had to organize a mental health awareness initiative next month, what would you do?
42. How do you measure the success of a team-building initiative?
43. What is the most stressful part of your job when Q4/Holiday gifting season arrives?
44. Have you ever hired an external facilitator for a virtual event? Was it worth the cost?
45. Tell me about a corporate gift you received personally that you actually kept and loved.
46. What usually happens to the corporate swag (mugs, pens) you give out?
47. How important is sustainability or eco-friendliness in your corporate purchasing?
48. Have you ever cancelled a vendor contract because their logistics were too messy?
49. What is the hardest part about engaging introverted employees during team events?
50. If you had a magic wand, what would the perfect employee gifting platform look like?

## Segment 3: Independent Designers & Creators (Questions 51-75)
**Focus: Monetization limits, supply chain fears, admin burnout, and passive income.**
51. Walk me through how you currently make money from your crochet audience.
52. Tell me about the last time you launched a new digital pattern. How did it perform?
53. What is the most frustrating part of selling digital PDFs on Etsy or Ravelry?
54. Have your followers ever asked if they could just buy the finished item from you?
55. Have they ever asked where to buy the exact yarn you used?
56. Tell me about a time you tried to sell a physical, finished item. How did the shipping go?
57. Have you ever considered manufacturing physical DIY kits of your designs?
58. What was the exact barrier that stopped you from launching a physical kit?
59. How much capital do you think it would take to launch your own kit?
60. Walk me through a typical week. What percentage of your time is spent actually designing?
61. What percentage of your time is spent on admin, answering DMs, or marketing?
62. Tell me about the worst customer service experience you've had to deal with.
63. Have you ever done an affiliate deal with a yarn company? How was the payout?
64. If you had to pack 50 boxes in your living room tomorrow, how would you feel?
65. Have you ever had a design go viral but felt you couldn't fully monetize the spike in traffic?
66. What scares you most about dealing with suppliers, shipping, and inventory?
67. How protective are you of your intellectual property and pattern rights?
68. Tell me about a creator platform or tool that you absolutely love using.
69. What is the most tedious part of sourcing yarn for a new prototype?
70. Would you rather make 100% of the profit but pack boxes all day, or make 15% and do nothing?
71. Have you ever felt burnt out by the constant need to pump out new digital content?
72. How do you currently track which of your designs are the most popular?
73. Have you ever had a package get lost in the mail? Who dealt with it?
74. What is your ultimate goal as a creator for the next 3 years?
75. If a logistics company approached you today, what is the first question you would ask them?

## General Discovery, Pricing & Closing (Questions 76-100)
**Focus: Willingness to pay, alternatives, and uncovering blind spots.**
76. What is the most you've ever paid to solve [Specific Problem]?
77. Tell me about the last time you paid for a premium convenience service (e.g., Uber, Glovo).
78. What software or tools do you currently use to hack together a solution for this?
79. If that workaround disappeared tomorrow, what would you do?
80. How much time does your current workaround take you every week?
81. If you could buy back that time, how much would it be worth to you in KES?
82. Have you ever tried a product that claimed to solve this but failed? What went wrong?
83. Who else in your life/company deals with this exact same problem?
84. How do you usually discover new products in this space? (Instagram, word of mouth?)
85. Tell me about a brand you trust completely. What did they do to earn that trust?
86. When buying online, what is your biggest fear before hitting the checkout button?
87. How important is same-day delivery to your purchasing decisions?
88. Have you ever abandoned a cart online? What was the exact reason?
89. What is the most annoying part of receiving deliveries via Boda Boda currently?
90. If you had a magic wand and could fix one thing about [Process], what would it be?
91. What is the worst advice you've ever received regarding [Topic]?
92. Tell me about a time you felt completely overwhelmed by a creative task.
93. What is the absolute minimum feature a product must have for you to even consider it?
94. How much does a solution like this *feel* like it should cost?
95. At what price would you consider this product so cheap that you would doubt its quality?
96. At what price would you consider it too expensive to even consider?
97. Is there anything I should have asked you today but didn't?
98. Based on what we discussed, who is one other person you think I should talk to?
99. Would you be willing to introduce me to them?
100. If I build a solution that solves exactly what we discussed, can I contact you to be our first beta tester?
