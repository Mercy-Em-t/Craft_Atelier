# Craft Atelier: User Personas (Early Adopters)

To ensure we build features that actually resonate, we have developed 2 distinct personas for each of our 3 primary customer segments. These represent our most critical early adopters.

---

## Segment 1: The "Craft-Curious" Beginner (B2C)

### Persona 1A: The Burned-Out Professional ("Screen-Fatigued Sarah")
* **Personal Background:** 28 years old, single, lives in Nairobi. Works as a Data Analyst or mid-level corporate manager. She spends 10+ hours a day staring at spreadsheets and Slack.
* **Technical Background:** Highly tech-savvy. Uses complex software daily, orders everything online (UberEats, Glovo), and expects seamless digital checkouts.
* **Relevant Experiences:** She recently tried a "Paint & Sip" class and loved the tactile feeling of creating something physical. However, when she tried to learn knitting from YouTube, she bought the wrong needles and gave up after 20 minutes of frustration.
* **Drivers & Goals:** Her primary goal is *mental decompression*. She doesn't necessarily care about becoming a master crafter; she just wants an activity that forces her to put her phone away for 2 hours in the evening.
* **Problems & Pain Points:** 
  * Going to a chaotic downtown market to buy yarn gives her anxiety.
  * She hates watching YouTube tutorials where the instructor's hands move too fast or go out of frame.
  * She needs a "guaranteed win"—if the project is too hard, she will abandon it.

### Persona 1B: The Trend-Chasing Gen Z ("Pinterest Patty")
* **Personal Background:** 21 years old, university student. Highly active on TikTok and Pinterest. She follows fashion trends closely and loves the "handmade, chunky-knit" aesthetic currently going viral online.
* **Technical Background:** Social media native. Extremely fluent in TikTok, Instagram, and mobile-first e-commerce. Zero tolerance for clunky websites.
* **Relevant Experiences:** She saw an influencer wearing a chunky crochet shoulder bag and immediately wanted one. She looked at the price ($150) and thought, "I could make that myself."
* **Drivers & Goals:** Her primary goal is *social validation and aesthetics*. She wants to create a beautiful, trendy accessory she can wear and post on her Instagram story saying "I made this."
* **Problems & Pain Points:** 
  * Traditional crafting feels like a "grandma" hobby; she only wants modern, trendy patterns.
  * She has a low attention span; she wants instant gratification and will not read a 10-page written pattern.
  * She is highly price-sensitive but will pay a premium if the packaging/unboxing experience is "aesthetic" enough to film for TikTok.

## Segment 2: The Corporate / Event Buyer (B2B)

### Persona 2A: The Remote Culture Builder ("HR Manager Mark")
* **Personal Background:** 38 years old, HR Director at a rapidly growing Fintech startup with a hybrid/remote workforce. Tasked with maintaining company culture.
* **Technical Background:** Proficient with B2B SaaS tools (Workday, Slack, Zoom). Expects automated invoicing, PDF receipts, and bulk CSV uploads.
* **Relevant Experiences:** He organized a virtual "Zoom Escape Room" last quarter and employee feedback was terrible ("Zoom fatigue"). He realizes employees want physical, tangible gifts that show the company cares about their mental health.
* **Drivers & Goals:** His goal is to execute a highly praised Wellness Month initiative with zero logistical headaches, proving ROI to the executive team.
* **Problems & Pain Points:** 
  * Collecting current home addresses for 75 remote employees is a nightmare.
  * Dealing with vendors who fail to deliver on time makes him look bad to the CEO.
  * Needs to pay via corporate bank transfer or get an official E-TIMS receipt; cannot just send money to a random personal M-Pesa number.

### Persona 2B: The Boutique Event Planner ("Event Evelyn")
* **Personal Background:** 32 years old, Founder of a boutique PR and Event Management agency. She organizes influencer retreats, bridal showers, and high-end brand activations.
* **Technical Background:** Moderate. Uses Canva, Excel, and WhatsApp Business heavily. Values human-centric communication over automated dashboards.
* **Relevant Experiences:** She recently hosted a brand launch where the scheduled activity fell flat. She is constantly hunting for unique, interactive "activations" that get attendees off their phones and talking to each other.
* **Drivers & Goals:** She wants to provide her clients with an unforgettable, premium experience. She needs activities that look beautiful when photographed.
* **Problems & Pain Points:** 
  * Finding reliable local vendors who can provide 30 identical, high-quality kits on short notice is nearly impossible.
  * She needs white-labeling options (the ability to stick her client's logo on the boxes).
  * Cannot risk missing parts (e.g., if one box is missing a crochet hook, it ruins the event for that one VIP attendee).

## Segment 3: The Independent Designer (Creator Economy)

### Persona 3A: The Viral Crocheter ("Creator Chloe")
* **Personal Background:** 25 years old. Full-time content creator with 80k followers on Instagram. She designs stunning, modern crochet bags and posts viral Reels showing them off.
* **Technical Background:** Expert at video editing (CapCut) and social media algorithms. Uses Shopify or Etsy for basic digital downloads. Low understanding of physical supply chains.
* **Relevant Experiences:** She posts a video of a new bag and gets 500 comments asking, "Where can I buy this exact bag?!" She tries to make them by hand but burns out after making 5 bags in a week.
* **Drivers & Goals:** She wants to monetize her massive audience and create a passive income stream so she can focus solely on designing new items, not fulfilling orders.
* **Problems & Pain Points:** 
  * She has no capital to buy 1,000 skeins of yarn from Turkey to build her own kits.
  * The idea of printing shipping labels, packing boxes, and arguing with couriers terrifies her.
  * She is afraid someone else will steal her design and mass-produce it before she can.

### Persona 3B: The Technical Pattern Writer ("Side-Hustle Sam")
* **Personal Background:** 40 years old, stay-at-home parent. Writes incredibly detailed, flawless crochet patterns and sells the PDFs on Ravelry for $5 each.
* **Technical Background:** Proficient in desktop publishing (InDesign, Word). Not very active on social media; relies on SEO and word-of-mouth in niche forums.
* **Relevant Experiences:** He has sold 2,000 copies of his PDF pattern over 3 years, making $10k. But he knows that if he sold a physical kit for $35, he could have made $70k.
* **Drivers & Goals:** Wants to legitimize his side-hustle into a real physical business without sacrificing his free time with his kids.
* **Problems & Pain Points:** 
  * He hates customer service and dealing with people asking basic questions.
  * He feels his $5 PDFs are undervalued compared to the hours of math and sizing he puts into them.
  * He lacks the marketing skills to launch a physical product on his own, needing a partner (Craft Atelier) who already has the storefront traffic.
