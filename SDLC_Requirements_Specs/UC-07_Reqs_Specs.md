# UC-07: Scan & Verify BOM

**Kano Priority:** 3. Must-Be

---

## Requirement 1
> **The Warehouse Packer physically places every required yarn skein and hook into the shipping box.**

### System Specifications
1. The System shall render a digital checklist matching the Bill of Materials retrieved from the `kits` database.
2. The System shall register a touch event on a list item and immediately alter its CSS state to green (verified).
3. The System shall prevent the order status from moving to PACKED until the array of verified items equals the required BOM array.
4. The System shall persist checklist state changes via WebSocket to prevent data loss on tablet refresh.

### World-Machine Interface Mapping
- **Environmental Phenomenon (The World):** The physical movement of yarn from a warehouse bin into a cardboard box.
- **Interface Phenomenon (The Machine):** The capacitive touch events generated by the Packer's finger on the tablet screen.

### Assumptions (Domain Properties)
*For the System meeting the Specification to successfully satisfy the Requirement, the following must hold true in the real world:*
- **A1:** When the Packer taps the screen, they have genuinely placed the item in the box (not just bypassing the UI).
- **A2:** The tablet hardware registers touch events accurately without phantom touches.
