# Solution-Fit Interview: Hypotheses & Questions

Based on the Mom-Test methodology and our specific UI prototypes, we formulated the following hypotheses and questions to validate our problem-solution fit.

## Falsifiable Hypotheses
1. **Hypothesis 1:** Beginners quit physical craft projects primarily because they cannot translate 2D written text into 3D hand movements.
2. **Hypothesis 2:** Left-handed beginners experience a disproportionately higher failure rate due to right-handed video bias.
3. **Hypothesis 3:** If offered an instant "SOS" video-call button in the UI, users will press it rather than abandon the project.

## Interview Questions
*Note: During the interview, users were shown the printed UI Sketches of the "Storefront Checkout" and "Interactive Tutorial" screens.*

1. Tell me about the last time you bought a DIY kit or tried to learn a physical hobby. What happened?
2. When you hit a roadblock in a tutorial video, exactly what did you do next? (Did you search elsewhere, pause, or quit?)
3. Are you left-handed or right-handed? If left-handed, how has that impacted your learning in the past?
4. *(Show Prototype Screen 2)* Walk me through how you would interact with this screen if you were completely stuck on a crochet stitch.
5. What are your immediate thoughts when you see the large "SOS" button? What do you expect to happen if you press it?
