# Quality Attributes (Non-Functional Requirements)

To ensure the Craft Atelier platform meets the expectations of our key stakeholders (Customers, Warehouse Staff, B2B Clients, and Creators), we have hypothesized the following 15 Quality Attributes as the most critical to the system's success. 

*Note: Target metrics are intentionally omitted in this phase; only units of measure are defined.*

---

### 1. Payment Latency (Performance)
* **Description:** The time delay between a customer clicking "Pay via M-Pesa" on the Storefront and the STK push prompt physically appearing on their mobile device. High latency here causes cart abandonment and duplicate charge attempts.
* **Unit of Measure:** Milliseconds (ms) or Seconds (s).

### 2. UI Responsiveness (Performance)
* **Description:** The speed at which the Warehouse Packer's tablet updates visually (e.g., flashing green) after they tap to verify an item on the Bill of Materials. If this lags, the packer's physical rhythm is broken.
* **Unit of Measure:** Milliseconds (ms).

### 3. System Availability (Uptime)
* **Description:** The proportion of time the storefront and Supabase backend are operational and accessible to process sales, particularly critical during sudden viral traffic spikes generated by TikTok creators.
* **Unit of Measure:** Percentage (%).

### 4. Concurrency (Data Integrity)
* **Description:** The system's ability to handle multiple simultaneous checkout attempts for the exact same physical inventory item (e.g., two people trying to buy the last ball of red yarn at the exact same time) without overselling.
* **Unit of Measure:** Number of race-condition errors or oversold items per thousand transactions.

### 5. Security (Financial Authentication)
* **Description:** The rigor of validation applied to asynchronous M-Pesa callbacks to ensure bad actors cannot spoof successful payment payloads to receive free physical kits.
* **Unit of Measure:** Number of unauthorized data injections / spoofing success rates.

### 6. Data Privacy
* **Description:** The enforcement of Row Level Security (RLS) in the Creator Portal to ensure Independent Creators can see their sales volume, but are strictly blocked from seeing the Personally Identifiable Information (PII) of the buyers.
* **Unit of Measure:** Number of PII data leak incidents.

### 7. Traceability (Auditability)
* **Description:** The completeness of the system's chronological logging for physical chain of custody—specifically ensuring every package has a recorded timestamp for packing, dispatch, and Delivery PIN validation.
* **Unit of Measure:** Percentage (%) of completed orders containing an unbroken, end-to-end audit log.

### 8. Interoperability
* **Description:** The system's ability to seamlessly communicate and exchange data with external third-party APIs (Safaricom M-Pesa, WhatsApp Business API, KRA e-TIMS) without integration failures.
* **Unit of Measure:** API failure rate (percentage of dropped requests/webhooks).

### 9. Accessibility
* **Description:** How easily the interactive video tutorial and pattern UI can be navigated by users with specific physical constraints, particularly left-handed crafters and visually impaired users.
* **Unit of Measure:** WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) Compliance Level (e.g., A, AA, AAA).

### 10. Usability (Learnability)
* **Description:** The ease with which a completely non-technical warehouse employee can pick up the Ops Tablet, understand the Kanban board, and successfully scan a Bill of Materials on their first day without training.
* **Unit of Measure:** Time to task completion (minutes) or Error Rate per 100 scans.

### 11. Maintainability (Configurability)
* **Description:** The ease and speed with which the System Admin can alter core business logic (e.g., updating the M-Pesa Till number or pausing all orders) via the Admin Hub without requiring a developer to push new code.
* **Unit of Measure:** Time (minutes/seconds) required to implement and deploy a configuration change.

### 12. Robustness (Network Tolerance)
* **Description:** The ability of the Dispatch and Packer tablets to gracefully handle micro-dropouts in warehouse WiFi, queueing up their item scans locally and syncing to Supabase once the connection is restored, rather than crashing.
* **Unit of Measure:** Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) or offline sync success rate (%).

### 13. Recoverability
* **Description:** How quickly and accurately the database can be restored to a stable state if an Admin accidentally runs a destructive query or a bad deployment corrupts the `orders` ledger.
* **Unit of Measure:** Time to Recovery (RTO - Hours/Minutes) and Data Loss Volume (RPO - Hours/Minutes of lost transactions).

### 14. Scalability (Load Capacity)
* **Description:** The backend's ability to gracefully handle extreme surges in database read/write requests without degrading performance when an Independent Creator launches a highly anticipated modular SKU.
* **Unit of Measure:** Requests Per Second (RPS) or Concurrent Connections.

### 15. Observability (Support Triage)
* **Description:** The depth of system state information instantly provided to a Support Agent when a user clicks the "Panic Button" (e.g., automatically capturing the exact video timestamp and row the user was stuck on).
* **Unit of Measure:** Percentage (%) of support tickets containing automated system-state context.
