# Craft Atelier: Comprehensive Stakeholder Analysis (Expanded to 40)

## Part 1: Stakeholder Profiles (1 to 20 - Core Ecosystem)

### 1. The "Craft-Curious" Beginner (B2C Customer)
* **Demographics:** Age 18-35. Urban professionals/students. Goal: Find a screen-free hobby to decompress.
* **Type:** Primary External User
* **Contact:** Instagram/TikTok surveys.
* **Information to Elicit:** Price sensitivity, unboxing friction, points of project abandonment.

### 2. The Corporate HR Manager (B2B Buyer)
* **Demographics:** Corporate administrators. Goal: Boost remote team morale without shipping headaches.
* **Type:** Primary External Decision Maker
* **Contact:** LinkedIn outreach.
* **Information to Elicit:** Budget per head, corporate reporting requirements, lead time needed.

### 3. Independent Crochet Designer (Creator/Partner)
* **Demographics:** Solopreneurs, highly creative. Goal: Monetize their audience passively via physical kits.
* **Type:** Secondary External Partner
* **Contact:** Instagram DMs, Ravelry.
* **Information to Elicit:** Royalty expectations, IP protection concerns.

### 4. The Gifter
* **Demographics:** Age 20-50. Buying for someone else. Goal: Look thoughtful without doing the work.
* **Type:** Primary External Buyer
* **Contact:** Checkout surveys.
* **Information to Elicit:** Need for gift wrapping, custom notes, or delayed shipping.

### 5. Warehouse Packer
* **Demographics:** Manual labor background. Goal: Pack boxes fast and accurately.
* **Type:** Primary Internal User
* **Contact:** Warehouse observation.
* **Information to Elicit:** Tablet UI friction, physical ergonomics of the packing station.

### 6. Ops & Dispatch Coordinator
* **Demographics:** High logistical expertise. Goal: Zero lost packages, efficient rider routing.
* **Type:** Primary Internal User
* **Contact:** Daily stand-ups.
* **Information to Elicit:** Rider batching difficulties, physical staging space limits.

### 7. Boda Boda Rider (Last-Mile Logistics)
* **Demographics:** Gig economy workers. Goal: Complete deliveries quickly to earn cash.
* **Type:** Secondary External
* **Contact:** WhatsApp groups, warehouse pickup.
* **Information to Elicit:** GPS accuracy, PIN handoff friction.

### 8. Business Owner / CEO
* **Demographics:** Entrepreneur. Goal: Scale revenue, minimize overhead.
* **Type:** Primary Internal Sponsor
* **Contact:** Direct review.
* **Information to Elicit:** Core KPIs, expansion thresholds.

### 9. Inventory & Procurement Manager
* **Demographics:** Supply chain expert. Goal: Prevent stockouts while keeping storage costs low.
* **Type:** Secondary Internal User
* **Contact:** Weekly syncs.
* **Information to Elicit:** Supplier lead times, physical vs digital stock discrepancies.

### 10. Local Yarn Wholesaler
* **Demographics:** Established local merchants. Goal: Secure recurring bulk orders.
* **Type:** Secondary External Vendor
* **Contact:** In-person visits.
* **Information to Elicit:** Bulk discount tiers, import tax effects on pricing.

### 11. International Yarn Manufacturer (Turkey/China)
* **Demographics:** Overseas factory reps. Goal: Fulfill massive shipping containers.
* **Type:** Tertiary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Alibaba, Email.
* **Information to Elicit:** Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs), shipping times to Mombasa.

### 12. Packaging Supplier (Boxes & Branding)
* **Demographics:** Local print shops. Goal: Provide custom boxes.
* **Type:** Secondary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Phone, email proofs.
* **Information to Elicit:** Print clarity for QR codes, volume pricing.

### 13. Safaricom / M-Pesa Merchant Rep
* **Demographics:** Corporate telecom. Goal: API compliance.
* **Type:** High-Power External
* **Contact:** Support tickets.
* **Information to Elicit:** API rate limits, chargeback handling.

### 14. Angel Investor / Financial Backer
* **Demographics:** High financial literacy. Goal: 10x ROI.
* **Type:** High-Power Internal/External Sponsor
* **Contact:** Quarterly reports.
* **Information to Elicit:** Required KPIs (CAC, LTV) for next funding round.

### 15. Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)
* **Demographics:** Regulatory body. Goal: Ensure tax collection.
* **Type:** High-Power External Regulatory
* **Contact:** e-TIMS portal.
* **Information to Elicit:** Required CSV export formatting.

### 16. Customer Support Agent
* **Demographics:** Empathetic, tech-savvy. Goal: Resolve tickets quickly.
* **Type:** Secondary Internal User
* **Contact:** Slack.
* **Information to Elicit:** Top reasons for refunds, common points of customer confusion.

### 17. National Courier Account Manager (G4S / Fargo)
* **Demographics:** Corporate logistics. Goal: Nationwide shipping volume.
* **Type:** Secondary External Partner
* **Contact:** SLA negotiations.
* **Information to Elicit:** API waybill payload structures, damage handling.

### 18. Crafting Influencer / Affiliate Marketer
* **Demographics:** Social media personalities. Goal: Maximize commission.
* **Type:** Secondary External Partner
* **Contact:** Affiliate dashboard.
* **Information to Elicit:** Promo code requirements, payout frequency preferences.

### 19. Competitor (Traditional Craft Stores)
* **Demographics:** Retail owners. Goal: Retain foot traffic.
* **Type:** External Monitor
* **Contact:** Mystery shopping.
* **Information to Elicit:** Pricing models, beginner onboarding strategies.

### 20. The Experienced Hobbyist
* **Demographics:** Deep crafting expertise. Goal: Complex patterns, raw materials.
* **Type:** Tertiary External User
* **Contact:** Facebook groups.
* **Information to Elicit:** Demand for premium materials (merino wool) vs beginner kits.

## Part 1.5: The Extended Ecosystem (21 to 40)

### 21. Warehouse Landlord / Property Manager
* **Demographics:** Commercial real estate owner. Goal: Rent collection, building safety.
* **Type:** Secondary External
* **Contact:** Lease negotiations.
* **Information to Elicit:** Power grid stability, space expansion limits, insurance requirements.

### 22. Internet Service Provider (Safaricom Home/Zuku)
* **Demographics:** Telecom provider. Goal: Maintain SLA.
* **Type:** Tertiary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Customer support.
* **Information to Elicit:** Redundancy options if fiber optic cable is cut (since the Ops tablets require constant WiFi).

### 23. Quality Assurance (QA) Inspector
* **Demographics:** Detail-oriented internal staff. Goal: Catch missing items before the box is sealed.
* **Type:** Primary Internal
* **Contact:** Warehouse floor.
* **Information to Elicit:** Most commonly forgotten items, lighting conditions for checking yarn colors.

### 24. Reverse Logistics Courier
* **Demographics:** Specialized returns driver. Goal: Process rejected/returned boxes.
* **Type:** Secondary External
* **Contact:** App integration.
* **Information to Elicit:** Protocol for customers who aren't home during return pickups.

### 25. Workshop Facilitator / Live Event Host
* **Demographics:** Crafters who run physical "Sip & Crochet" events. Goal: Bulk order kits to use as curriculum for their classes.
* **Type:** Secondary B2B Customer
* **Contact:** Direct outreach.
* **Information to Elicit:** Do they need instructor-only guidebooks?

### 26. Social Media Manager
* **Demographics:** Creative digital native. Goal: Drive engagement and ad conversions.
* **Type:** Secondary Internal
* **Contact:** Marketing syncs.
* **Information to Elicit:** Which user-generated photos perform best as ads?

### 27. Intellectual Property (IP) Lawyer
* **Demographics:** Legal professional. Goal: Protect Craft Atelier and Creator patterns from being stolen/copied.

* **Contact:** Legal retainers.

### 28. Corporate Accountant / Bookkeeper
* **Demographics:** Financial expert. Goal: Keep books balanced.
* **Type:** Secondary Internal
* **Contact:** Monthly financial syncs.
* **Information to Elicit:** Integration requirements between Supabase financial logs and QuickBooks/Xero.

### 29. Payment Gateway Aggregator (Pesapal / Paystack)
* **Demographics:** Fintech rep. Goal: Process card payments for non-M-Pesa users.
* **Type:** Secondary External Partner
* **Contact:** API documentation.
* **Information to Elicit:** Settlement times to bank accounts, transaction fees.

### 30. Commercial Insurance Provider
* **Demographics:** Corporate broker. Goal: Sell policies.
* **Type:** Tertiary External
* **Contact:** Annual policy reviews.
* **Information to Elicit:** Fire/Theft coverage limits for the warehouse inventory.

### 31. Supabase Platform Engineers
* **Demographics:** Cloud database maintainers. Goal: Uptime.
* **Type:** High-Power External Dependency
* **Contact:** GitHub issues, status pages.
* **Information to Elicit:** Scheduled maintenance windows, Edge Function execution limits.

### 32. Front-End Hosting Provider (GitHub Pages / Vercel)
* **Demographics:** CDN providers. Goal: Serve HTML fast.
* **Type:** Secondary External Dependency
* **Contact:** Status pages.
* **Information to Elicit:** Bandwidth limits if the storefront goes viral.

### 33. Android SMS Webhook Developer
* **Demographics:** 3rd party app developer handling the M-Pesa Till SMS forwarding.
* **Type:** Secondary External Vendor
* **Contact:** App store support.
* **Information to Elicit:** Battery optimization rules that might kill the app in the background.

### 34. Custom Tools Manufacturer
* **Demographics:** Niche woodworking factories. Goal: Provide custom branded wooden crochet hooks.
* **Type:** Tertiary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Alibaba / Local artisans.
* **Information to Elicit:** Tooling costs, minimum orders for laser-engraved logos.

### 35. Graphic Designer / Box Illustrator
* **Demographics:** Freelancer. Goal: Create beautiful unboxing aesthetics.
* **Type:** Secondary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Upwork / Email.
* **Information to Elicit:** Color palettes, die-cut templates for the cardboard supplier.

### 36. Waste Management / Recycling Partner
* **Demographics:** Local municipal or private recycler. Goal: Collect warehouse waste.
* **Type:** Tertiary External
* **Contact:** Monthly collection schedules.
* **Information to Elicit:** Costs to haul away bulk cardboard scraps and discarded yarn.

### 37. Educational Institutions (Schools)
* **Demographics:** Art teachers, Principals. Goal: Buy curriculum-friendly craft kits for after-school programs.
* **Type:** Tertiary B2B Customer
* **Contact:** Direct sales to schools.
* **Information to Elicit:** Educational discount requirements, child-safety certifications needed for hooks.

### 38. Occupational Therapists / Clinics
* **Demographics:** Healthcare professionals. Goal: Use crochet kits for patients needing fine motor skill rehabilitation.
* **Type:** Niche B2B Customer
* **Contact:** Medical conferences.
* **Information to Elicit:** Specific yarn thickness requirements for arthritic patients.

### 39. Public Relations (PR) / Lifestyle Journalists
* **Demographics:** Writers for Kenyan lifestyle blogs/magazines. Goal: Write engaging stories about local startups.
* **Type:** Secondary External
* **Contact:** Press releases.
* **Information to Elicit:** What makes Craft Atelier a "news-worthy" story?

### 40. Local Delivery App Aggregators (Glovo / Sendy)
* **Demographics:** 3rd party logistics platforms. Goal: Route riders.
* **Type:** External Competitor/Alternative
* **Contact:** API integration docs.
* **Information to Elicit:** What happens if our direct Boda Boda network fails? Can we fall back on their API?

## Part 1.75: The Deep Ecosystem & Supply Chain (41 to 60)

### 41. Customs Clearing Agent (Mombasa Port)
* **Demographics:** Freight bureaucracy expert. Goal: Clear incoming yarn containers without paying demurrage fees.
* **Type:** High-Power External Vendor
* **Contact:** Direct hire/Agency.
* **Information to Elicit:** Bribes/friction at the port, exact HS codes for importing Turkish yarn to avoid higher tax brackets.

### 42. Hardware Supplier (Zippers & Clasps)
* **Demographics:** Industrial hardware vendors. Goal: Sell metal bag accessories.
* **Type:** Tertiary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Wholesale markets / Alibaba.
* **Information to Elicit:** Rust-resistance of metal rings, minimum order quantities for custom engraved zippers.

### 43. Print Shop Partner (Instruction Manuals)
* **Demographics:** Local commercial printers. Goal: Print the high-quality unboxing guide booklets.
* **Type:** Secondary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Email / Walk-in.
* **Information to Elicit:** Cost difference between glossy vs matte paper, lead time for 1,000 booklets.

### 44. Data Privacy Regulator (ODPC Kenya)
* **Demographics:** Government bureaucrats. Goal: Ensure customer phone numbers (M-Pesa data) are not leaked or sold.
* **Type:** High-Power Regulatory
* **Contact:** Official compliance portals.
* **Information to Elicit:** GDPR/Kenya Data Protection Act requirements for storing customer delivery addresses.

### 45. Cybersecurity Auditor / Pen-Tester
* **Demographics:** White-hat hackers. Goal: Find vulnerabilities in the Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) rules.
* **Type:** Secondary External Advisor
* **Contact:** Contracted via Upwork/Agencies.
* **Information to Elicit:** Can a malicious user forge an M-Pesa transaction payload to trick the database?

### 46. Supermarket / Retail Buyer (e.g., Carrefour/Naivas)
* **Demographics:** Corporate retail purchasers. Goal: Stock high-margin physical goods on retail shelves.
* **Type:** High-Power B2B Customer (Phase 3)
* **Contact:** Trade shows, corporate outreach.
* **Information to Elicit:** Shelf-space fees, required barcode (EAN/UPC) formats for the boxes.

### 47. Museum / Art Gallery Gift Shop Manager
* **Demographics:** Curation experts. Goal: Sell aesthetic, locally-themed craft kits to tourists.
* **Type:** Niche B2B Customer
* **Contact:** In-person meetings.
* **Information to Elicit:** Do they need "Kenya-themed" exclusive crochet designs (e.g., a Safari hat)?

### 48. International Diaspora Customer
* **Demographics:** Kenyans abroad or international expats. Goal: Buy kits from home or ship via DHL.
* **Type:** Niche B2C Customer
* **Contact:** Website international checkout.
* **Information to Elicit:** Willingness to pay $50+ for DHL shipping, customs friction in their home country.

### 49. Video Production Freelancer
* **Demographics:** Videographers/Editors. Goal: Shoot the cinematic, step-by-step unboxing tutorials.
* **Type:** Secondary External Contractor
* **Contact:** Freelance platforms.
* **Information to Elicit:** Cost per hour of edited footage, ability to shoot top-down macro shots of crochet hooks.

### 50. SEO Specialist / Crafting Blogger
* **Demographics:** Digital marketer. Goal: Capture Google search traffic for "How to crochet a bag".
* **Type:** Secondary Internal/External
* **Contact:** Marketing syncs.
* **Information to Elicit:** Which keyword phrases have the highest search volume but lowest competition in East Africa?

### 51. Local Business Podcast Host / Journalist
* **Demographics:** Media personalities. Goal: Interview the CEO about building a unique local startup.
* **Type:** Secondary External Media
* **Contact:** Twitter / PR Outreach.
* **Information to Elicit:** What is the "hook" of our startup story that makes their listeners tune in?

### 52. Business Bank Account Manager (FX Handler)
* **Demographics:** Corporate bankers (e.g., Equity/KCB). Goal: Handle large wire transfers for international supplier payments.
* **Type:** High-Power External
* **Contact:** Priority banking lines.
* **Information to Elicit:** USD/KES exchange rate spreads, delays in telegraphic transfers (TT) to China/Turkey.

### 53. Temporary Packaging Assemblers (Seasonal Labor)
* **Demographics:** Unskilled labor hired only during the Christmas rush. Goal: Earn daily wages folding cardboard boxes.
* **Type:** Secondary Internal User
* **Contact:** Local recruitment.
* **Information to Elicit:** How long does it take to train someone to fold the box correctly without tearing the cardboard?

### 54. Local Charity / NGO Partner
* **Demographics:** Social workers. Goal: Receive donated kits for youth empowerment or women's shelters.
* **Type:** Secondary External Partner
* **Contact:** CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) outreach.
* **Information to Elicit:** Can they use our excess/discontinued yarn inventory to teach vocational skills?

### 55. Environmental Auditor / Eco-Certification Body
* **Demographics:** Sustainability experts. Goal: Certify the business as "Zero Waste" or carbon neutral.
* **Type:** Secondary External Advisor
* **Contact:** Consultation.
* **Information to Elicit:** What exact metrics do we need to hit to put a "Certified Eco-Friendly" badge on our boxes?

### 56. 3D/AR Modeling Freelancer
* **Demographics:** Tech artist. Goal: Build the 3D models of the bags for the Augmented Reality app feature.
* **Type:** Tertiary External Contractor
* **Contact:** Upwork / ArtStation.
* **Information to Elicit:** File size optimization so the 3D models load instantly on mobile phones.

### 57. Freight Forwarder
* **Demographics:** Logistics brokers. Goal: Move the physical shipping container from the Turkish factory to the Mombasa port.
* **Type:** Secondary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Agency dispatch.
* **Information to Elicit:** Marine insurance costs, tracking visibility while the ship is on the ocean.

### 58. Fabric / Lining Supplier
* **Demographics:** Local textile merchants. Goal: Supply the inner cloth linings for the completed crochet bags.
* **Type:** Tertiary External Vendor
* **Contact:** Local markets.
* **Information to Elicit:** Can they pre-cut the fabric into the exact dimensions needed for our kits?

### 59. Labor Board / Trade Union
* **Demographics:** Government/Union officials. Goal: Ensure fair wages and working conditions for warehouse packers.
* **Type:** High-Power Regulatory
* **Contact:** Legal compliance checks.
* **Information to Elicit:** Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) rules regarding lighting and ergonomics in the packing room.

### 60. Platform Copycat Competitors
* **Demographics:** Other tech-savvy entrepreneurs. Goal: See our success and try to clone our exact fulfillment engine.
* **Type:** External Threat
* **Contact:** Zero contact (Monitoring).
* **Information to Elicit:** How fast can they replicate our supply chain? What is our "Unfair Advantage" they cannot steal (e.g., our Creator partnerships)?

## Part 2: Stakeholder Analysis Matrix (Power / Interest Grid)

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Power, High Interest** | **Manage Closely** | Business Owner, Corporate HR Managers, Angel Investors, Independent Designers, IP Lawyer, Accountant, Supermarket Buyers. |
