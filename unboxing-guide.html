Zero Frustration Engineering

The Pre-Started Piece

Beginners usually quit during the foundation chain because stitches curl and tighten. We already did the hard part for you.

Do Not Pull The Loose Tail!

Your working yarn is securely clipped into the neon marker. Do not tug the short tail.

Executing Row 1 (Single Crochet)

1

Insert Hook: Slide hook tip under both loops of the next open stitch along the top ridge.

2

Yarn Over: Catch the yarn with the hook beak and pull it through (you now have 2 loops on hook).

3

Complete Stitch: Catch yarn again and pull through both loops smoothly. One stitch done!

"Keep your loops loose enough to slide along the silver shaft of the hook."

Fold Line 2
Direct Maker Support

Stuck? Don't Unravel.

Every maker makes tight stitches on day one. If your work feels jammed or curved, do not pull your stitches out.

Golden Tension Check

Too tight? You are pulling the working yarn with your index finger. Relax your grip and let the hook do the sizing.

Lost count? Count the "V" shapes along the top rim. Each "V" is one stitch.
